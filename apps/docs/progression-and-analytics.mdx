# Follow progression and team trends

Use **Progression** for a swimmer’s best-time history and **Analytics** for coaching views across the team.

## Progression

Project Aqua keeps times with the team that imported them. Open a swimmer’s progression page to compare their best times by event and course without mixing results from another team.

You can also import times from a CSV when a full meet-result file is not available.

## Analytics

Analytics brings together views such as attendance, training volume, top times, and cut tracking. Use these views to spot progress and decide what deserves a closer coaching conversation.

<Tip>
  Import results promptly after a meet. The current best-time and standard views are only as complete as the result data the team has brought in.
</Tip>
